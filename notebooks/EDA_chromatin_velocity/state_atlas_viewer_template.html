How to read this view

Common PCA is trained on observed accessibility across all stages together. UMAP receives all retained PCA components, and its map is also trained jointly on observed states. Retrospectively fitted states and held-out peaks are placed into the same frozen maps. No chronological links, roots, or stage labels were supplied to the embedding. Lines connect matching peak identities only after embedding.

Colors identify actual developmental stages. The five intervals are 2, 2, 2, 3 and 5 hours; a longer screen segment need not represent a faster biological change. The interval table gives changes in measured relative CPM and specificity separately. Specificity is tau: 0 is equal accessibility across the selected celltypes and 1 is concentrated in one celltype; it is undefined when every coordinate is zero.

Every panel uses fixed celltype coordinates over its stated window. The 21-celltype six-stage panel is the primary view. Included celltype–stage combinations are present and contain at least 20 cells. Optic cup at 10 hpf is present but below this threshold, so its embedding uses 12–24 hpf. The two 12–24 hpf panels compare the effect of adding three celltypes, including optic cup. They are independently fitted coordinate systems: screen positions should not be compared across panels. The celltype curve and heatmap retain all 32 celltypes for inspection.

The searchable viewer is a display subset of the saved analysis sample. Background density is not an estimate of cell or peak abundance. Illustrative trajectories were deliberately selected by profile criteria, and do not measure program prevalence. Relative CPM cannot establish absolute genome-wide opening.
